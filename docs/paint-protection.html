How it runs

Four days, in order.

01

Intake & inspection

Paint depth readings, defect mapping under inspection lighting, and photographs of everything before we touch it. You get the same file we work from.

02

Decontamination & correction

Chemical and mechanical decontamination, then multi-stage correction as needed. Film and coating both go onto finished paint, never onto defects.

03

Installation

Film plotted to your exact model, wrapped past the edges, and relieved by hand where the pattern won't do it. Dust-controlled bay, controlled temperature.

04

Cure & handover

Coating cures under infrared, film settles overnight. Final inspection, aftercare walk-through, and your project goes into the archive.

Questions we answer on the phone

The honest answers.

How long does paint protection film actually last?

Seven to ten years on a car that's washed properly and kept out of permanent sun. XPEL's warranty runs ten years against yellowing, cracking, and delamination. Film on a daily driver in Metro Vancouver typically still looks right at the eight-year mark; the F-150 we mention above went past ten.

Do I need PPF, ceramic coating, or both?

They solve different problems. Film is physical armour — it stops rock chips and scratches. Coating is chemical — it repels water and contaminants and makes washing far easier, but it will not stop a stone. If you're choosing one, film on the front end is the higher-value purchase. Most people who do both put film on the impact zones and coating over everything.

What does it cost?

Front-end packages and full-vehicle coverage sit at very different ends of the range, and the honest driver of price is panel count and paint condition rather than the package name. Send us the year, make, and model and we'll come back with a real number, usually the same day. We'd rather quote your specific car than publish a figure that turns out to be wrong for it.

Can you put film on a car that's already been damaged?

Yes, but correction comes first. Film magnifies whatever is underneath it, and coating seals defects in permanently. If the paint has swirls or light scratches we'll correct them as part of the job. Deeper damage that needs paintwork, we'll tell you before we start.

How long will you have my car?

A front-end package is usually two days. Full vehicle film with correction and coating runs four to five. Coating needs to cure before the car goes back out in the rain, so we build that into the schedule rather than rushing handover.

Someone else installed my film and it looks wrong. Can you fix it?

Regularly. Lifting edges, visible seams, trapped dirt, and film that stops short of the panel edge are all things we redo. Removal is done with steam and takes time, but it doesn't harm factory paint.
